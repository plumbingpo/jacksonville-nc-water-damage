Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. Across Jacksonville, Jacksonville Restoration Squad answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Jacksonville

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Wastewater seeping from the sewer cleanout or pooling in the yard above the line
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
